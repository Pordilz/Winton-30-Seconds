Winton 30 Seconds

Teams

    How to play
    1. Split into teams. On your turn, one person is the describer.
    2. Roll the die first. Whatever it lands on (0, 1 or 2) comes off your score for that turn.
    3. Start the clock. The describer sees five terms and has to get their team to say them, in any order.
    4. No saying the term, any part of it, or "sounds like" / "rhymes with". No spelling it out either.
    5. Tap each term as your team gets it. When the time runs out, everyone checks the results.
    6. Points = terms guessed minus the die. First team to the target wins.
    7. Pass the phone to the next team's describer.
