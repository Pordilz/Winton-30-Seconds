    How to play
    1. Split into teams. On each turn, one person describes and the rest of their team guesses.
    2. Roll the die. Whatever it lands on (0, 1 or 2) comes off your score for that turn.
    3. Start the clock. The describer gets five terms and has 30 seconds to get the team to say them, in any order.
    4. No saying the term or any part of it, no "sounds like" or "rhymes with", no spelling it out.
    5. Tap a term, or swipe it right, the moment your team gets it. Cleared all five with time left? Deal the next card and keep going.
    6. When time's up, both teams check the card. Points are the terms guessed minus the die.
    7. First team to the target wins. With the Chaos die on, some rolls change the rules for that turn instead.

    Feed the deck

    A name, a place, a saying, a night nobody will let go of. If most of the group would get it, it goes in.

                    How the quiz works
                    1. Each turn, spin for a category, then the team on the phone gets one question with four answers.
                    2. A right answer is worth 500, plus up to 500 more the faster you lock it in.
                    3. Answer streaks pay: +100 for each right answer in a row after the first, up to +500.
                    4. Each team has one 50:50. It knocks out two wrong answers.
                    5. The last round is worth double. Most points at the end wins.
